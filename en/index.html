About Mente de Equipaje

03

You don't need to have all the answers to start a conversation.

Mente de Equipaje grew from a simple image: we all carry a suitcase, even if we cannot always see it. Inside we keep places, memories, people, fears, decisions and lessons.

This independent project brings together stories, reflections and conversations about what we experience, what we leave behind and what stays with us when we begin a new chapter. It is not only about travelling, but about everything life places in our suitcase.
